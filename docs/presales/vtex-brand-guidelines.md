## What it does

`vtex-brand-guidelines` applies VTEX's official visual identity and voice standards when generating documents, presentations, proposals, and other branded artifacts. It carries the brand's core personality traits (Bold, Trustworthy, and their balance), colors, typography, logo rules, and voice/tone, sourced from [brand.vtex.com](https://brand.vtex.com/), plus a reference for generating `.pptx` files that follow those rules.

## When to reach for it

Reaches for itself automatically on requests like "create a VTEX presentation", "make a VTEX slide deck", "apply VTEX brand guidelines", "generate a VTEX-branded document", "create a PPT following VTEX brand", "use VTEX colors and fonts", or "create a VTEX proposal" — anything that must follow VTEX's official brand standards.

## Prerequisites

None beyond the skill itself. `references/pptx-generation.md` documents the `.pptx` generation workflow used when the deliverable is a slide deck.
