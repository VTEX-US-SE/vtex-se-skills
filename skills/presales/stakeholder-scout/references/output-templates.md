## 1. Stakeholder map

Group by scope (regional vs. local) or by function, whichever tells the story better. One line of engagement guidance per person.

```
## [Account] — Stakeholder Map

### Regional / governance ([location])
| Person | Title | Role | Influence | Stance | LinkedIn | Engage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [Title] | [DMU role] | [H/M/L] | [stance] | [url] | [one-line play] |

### Local / working team ([location])
| Person | Title | Role | Influence | Stance | LinkedIn | Engage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [Title] | [DMU role] | [H/M/L] | [stance] | [url] | [one-line play] |

### Convergence points worth acting on
- [Name] — [CP-x]: [what it means for our approach]
- [Name] — [CP-x]: [what it means for our approach]

### Gaps / unconfirmed
- [Name] — [what's missing; how to resolve, e.g. "confirm via champion"]

**Net read:** [who to engage first and why — 1–2 sentences]
```

## 4. Bot Scout Slack update

Internal channel post. Casual and fun, but the intel is precise. Emojis welcome. **Always show to the user for review before posting.**

```
🕵️ **Bot Scout services update — [Account]** [relevant emoji]

Your friendly neighborhood spy-bot did some recon. Net read: [the one-sentence headline finding].

[Group A — e.g. Regional HQ ([location] — where the decision is governed):]
- [Name] — [Title] — [linkedin url]
- [Name] — [Title] — [linkedin url]

[Group B — e.g. Local team (owns requirements + delivery):]
- [Name] — [Title] ([note, e.g. our current contact / technical gatekeeper]) — [linkedin url]

🔓 Warm path: [Target] is a [2nd-degree] connection — mutuals [colleagues] → direct intro to the [decision level].

🔍 Still on the case: [unconfirmed names] — will place them via the client.

Net move: [engage X locally, push for Y early — one line]. 🕵️
```

Tone notes:
- Keep it scannable — short groups, one line each.
- Lead with the headline (where the decision really sits), not the full roster.
- The warm-path line is the punchline — make it land.
- If the deal is sensitive, dial the playfulness down and keep it factual.
