# Presales

Skills used in pre-sales work: solution design, demo building, and similar customer-facing
preparation. RFP response is out of scope here — that's being handled together with Atlas,
separately.

- **[vtex-brand-guidelines](./vtex-brand-guidelines/SKILL.md)**: applies VTEX's official visual identity and voice standards when generating documents, presentations, proposals, and other branded artifacts. Docs: [docs/presales/vtex-brand-guidelines.md](../../docs/presales/vtex-brand-guidelines.md).
- **[stakeholder-scout](./stakeholder-scout/SKILL.md)**: turns scattered deal signals into a decision-ready stakeholder map for a commerce deal, account, or RFP — career history, convergence points, DMU role, influence, warm-intro paths. Docs: [docs/presales/stakeholder-scout.md](../../docs/presales/stakeholder-scout.md).
- **[solution-design](./solution-design/SKILL.md)**: produces a technical, persuasive, pre-sales Solution Design document for an RFP/RFQ opportunity — section planning, architecture decisions, case study research, RFP Coverage Appendix. Requires `vams-to-miro` installed separately (external prerequisite, not bundled here — see docs). Docs: [docs/presales/solution-design.md](../../docs/presales/solution-design.md).
